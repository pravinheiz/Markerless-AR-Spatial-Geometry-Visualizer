Markerless AR Spatial Geometry Visualizer

A zero-hardware-barrier, browser-based Augmented Reality visualizer that empowers users to synthesize, manipulate, analyze, and paint 3D spatial geometry in mid-air using real-time computer vision hand tracking.

🖐️

Markerless Hand Tracking

No controllers or AR glasses required. Uses standard webcam feed via MediaPipe ML pipeline.

✨

Tony Stark 6-DOF Control

Intuitive wrist-tilt orientation (Pitch, Yaw, Roll) and 2-hand hologram zoom scaling.

📐

Real-Time Spatial Telemetry

Instant mathematical calculation of Volume, Surface Area, Bounding Dimensions, and Laser Ruler distances.

THE PROBLEM & CHALLENGE

Overcoming the 2D Flatland Barrier in Spatial Computing

Traditional CAD and geometry visualization software rely on flat 2D monitors and mouse interfaces, severely restricting spatial intuition and volumetric comprehension.

🖥️

2D Screen Flattening

Loss of natural 3D depth perception when learning spatial geometry, Euler solids, and volumetric math.

🥽

Expensive AR/VR Hardware

Meta Quest, Apple Vision Pro, and HoloLens cost thousands of dollars, creating severe accessibility barriers for students.

⚙️

Cumbersome Physical Markers

Traditional marker-based AR requires printed QR codes or fiducial tags, restricting freeform spatial interaction.

INNOVATIVE SOLUTION

The Markerless Web-AR Architecture

Our system delivers an Iron Man-style spatial holographic laboratory directly inside any modern web browser using client-side AI and WebGL rendering.

🌐

Universal Web Accessibility

Runs natively via WebGL and WebAssembly with zero software installation or plugins.

⚡

Sub-20ms Real-Time Vision

MediaPipe neural net computes 21 3D hand keypoints per hand at 60 FPS directly in the browser.

🎯

Hysteresis & Anti-Jitter Filtering

One-Euro exponential smoothing eliminates hand jitter and accidental drawing triggers during hand waving.

TECHNICAL ARCHITECTURE

Three-Tiered Synchronous Render Pipeline

The application architecture coordinates three distinct rendering and compute layers seamlessly in real time.

📹

Layer 0: Mirror Video Feed

Raw camera stream processed with scaleX(-1) mirror correction for a natural selfie AR experience.

🧊

Layer 1: Three.js Spatial Engine

Transparent WebGL canvas rendering 3D primitives, dynamic lights, Catmull-Rom tubes, and spatial grids.

⚡

Layer 2: Holographic HUD Overlay

2D canvas rendering glowing bone connections, Oculus fingertip cursors, and live distance laser telemetry.
